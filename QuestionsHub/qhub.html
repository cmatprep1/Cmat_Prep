🚀 Welcome to the CodeWar 1.0 Prep Dashboard

This site is built specifically to give you hints about the type of questions you’ll face on Day 1 of CodeWar 1.0.

It’s not a full practice platform — it’s a guided space designed to help you understand what kind of basic programming questions to expect so you don’t walk into the event feeling lost or unprepared.

🧩 What can you do here?

  • Browse through a variety of beginner-friendly programming questions.
  • Try solving them on your own — even partial attempts will help!
  • If stuck, copy the question and ask ChatGPT for hints, explanations, or even code suggestions.
  • Use the question formats to train your brain for how problems will be asked in CodeWar 1.0.
🔍 The goal is simple: Make sure you don’t feel like “I don’t know anything.”

We’re not testing you here — we’re preparing you. This dashboard is your first step toward building problem-solving confidence.

🛑 Don't just rely on this site only.

To strengthen your preparation, explore more problems, techniques, and concepts from other reliable sources:

  • GeeksForGeeks
  • LeetCode
  • HackerRank
  • Codeforces

We recommend reviewing common coding problems, basic algorithms, and core data structures. These platforms offer hands-on challenges to sharpen your skills.

🤝 Work with teammates, share doubts, discuss logic, and learn together — teamwork is a vital part of CodeWar too!

🌱 Final Note: It’s okay not to know everything — the point is to start. And with this dashboard, you already have. Keep exploring, keep practicing — you’ve got this!
